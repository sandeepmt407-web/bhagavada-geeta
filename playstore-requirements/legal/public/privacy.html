Privacy Policy

Bhagavad Gita  ·  com.theops.bhagvadgeeta

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This app does not collect, store or transmit any personal information about you. There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no analytics of any kind.

In short

  • No sign-in, no account, no personal data collected.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits of any kind.
  • Your settings, bookmarks and reading progress stay on your phone.
  • The one thing that can leave your device is the verse text, and only if you choose an online voice for narration. This is explained in full below.

What is stored, and where

The app remembers a small number of things so that it works the way you left it: the language and translation you chose, whether narration and background music are on, the reading speed and voice you selected, your text size, the verse you last read, your bookmarks, which verses you have read, and whether you asked for a daily verse and at what hour.

All of it is written to your phone's own app storage. None of it is sent anywhere, none of it is readable by us, and all of it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Narration and your phone's speech engine

The app does not ship recorded audio. Verses are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine that is already installed on your phone — usually Speech Services by Google. The app passes the text of the verse to that engine and the engine produces the sound.

Some voices work offline and some do not. A voice marked “Needs a connection” is synthesised on the provider's servers, which means the text of the verse is sent from your phone to that provider in order to be spoken. The app ships with such a voice selected by default because it is the most natural sounding one available on most phones.

That transmission is made by your phone's speech engine, not by the app, and it is governed by the privacy policy of whoever supplies that engine — for Speech Services by Google, that is Google's privacy policy. Only the verse text is involved. Nothing about you is attached to it by this app.

If you would rather nothing left your device at all, open Language & Narration in the app and choose any voice marked “Works offline”, or turn narration off entirely. Both settings are yours and the app will remember them.

Daily verse notifications

If you turn on the daily verse, the notification is scheduled by your phone and delivered by your phone. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no push service, and no message is delivered from us to you. Turning the setting off cancels everything already scheduled.

Sharing a verse

When you share a verse, the app draws a picture of it and saves that picture to your phone's Pictures folder, then hands it to whichever app you pick from the share sheet. The app does not upload it. What happens to it after you choose an app is governed by that app.

Permissions, and why they exist

  • Internet. Required so that an online narration voice can work. The app itself makes no network requests of its own: it fetches nothing, reports nothing, and checks for nothing. The entire scripture is inside the download.
  • Notifications. Only used for the daily verse, and only if you turn it on.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, children included.
